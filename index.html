FEIE Calculator for U.S. Expats

See what you’d owe with the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion.

The Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) lets qualifying U.S. citizens and green card holders living abroad exclude up to $130,000 of foreign earned income for 2025 and $132,900 for 2026. Answer up to 10 questions to see whether it applies to you, an estimate of any U.S. income tax you’d still owe, and whether the Foreign Tax Credit may suit you better.

  • Free
  • No email needed to see your result
  • Uses 2025 and 2026 IRS figures

How this estimate works

The calculator uses the IRS figures for the tax year you choose. The FEIE takes your foreign pay out of U.S. income tax, up to the yearly limit. Any pay above the limit is taxed at the rate it would face if nothing were excluded, which is the IRS stacking rule. If you pay foreign income tax, the Foreign Tax Credit can lower what’s left.

This calculator gives an estimate only, based on your answers and published IRS figures. It isn’t tax advice. Your U.S. tax depends on your full situation, and a Greenback accountant with expertise in expat returns can confirm it.
