FEIE Calculator: Estimate Your U.S. Tax Abroad

The Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) lets qualifying U.S. citizens and green card holders living abroad exclude up to $130,000 of foreign earned income for 2025 and $132,900 for 2026. Answer up to 10 questions to see whether the FEIE applies to you, an estimate of any U.S. income tax you'd still owe, and whether the Foreign Tax Credit may suit you better.

How this estimate works

The calculator uses the IRS figures for the tax year you choose. The FEIE takes your foreign pay out of U.S. tax up to the yearly limit. Any pay above the limit is taxed at the rate it would face if nothing were excluded, which is the IRS stacking rule.

This calculator gives an estimate only, based on your answers and published IRS figures. It isn't tax advice. Your actual U.S. tax depends on your full situation, and a Greenback accountant with expertise in expat returns can confirm it.
